      About Parker

      Our promise is simple. Every movement in your kitchen should feel extraordinary.

      Parker began with a belief that the hardware inside a kitchen deserves the same care as the stone, wood and light around it. The drawer that glides. The door that comes to rest without a sound. The corner that finally gives back its space.

      We are setting out to make fittings worthy of the finest homes: engineered with precision, finished with restraint, and made to be relied on every single day. This is only the beginning.

      Hover or tap a door to open our promiseTap a door to open our promise

      Precision you can feel

      Every drawer, door and pull-out should start lightly and finish softly. We design each movement so it feels the same on the thousandth use as on the first.

      Design that belongs

      Hardware should suit the room it lives in. Clean lines and considered finishes that disappear into the cabinetry until the moment you reach for them.

      Craft that endures

      We would rather make fewer things, properly. Every fitting added to our range has to earn its place by working well, day after day.
